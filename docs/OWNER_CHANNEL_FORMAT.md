# Owner channel format (standing rule, set 2026-10-23)

Everything owner-facing goes through the Chief of Agent Staff (pane `w9:pS`), which forwards it in
exactly this form:

- **2 to 4 sentences** of plain English: what happened, what it means, what is next.
- **At most 4 bullets**, and only these kinds: **decisions, numbers, blockers, dates**.
- **No** logs, hashes, filenames (unless the filename *is* the question), narration of what was
  checked, sign-offs, or message delimiters.
- Anything longer is returned with one line naming what to cut.

## Why
The owner's stated reason: a filter against agents being "voicey/gabby", and executive-grade
compression. Both the VP and every lane had drifted long; the drift was invisible because nobody
was measuring length against a bar.

## What does NOT change
Lanes keep sending **full technical detail to the VP** - receipts, hashes, case counts, commands,
case-by-case evidence. That detail is what makes work re-runnable and it is what the VP verifies
against. The compression happens once, on the way to the owner.

Long-form belongs in: repo docs, the dashboard, and each lane's own receipts file.

## The VP's own failure this rule corrects
Several owner briefs on 2026-10-23 were 40-80 lines, with verification narration the owner had not
asked for. The rule applies to the VP first.
